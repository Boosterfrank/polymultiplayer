Car Switcher

By Orangy

Contributors: CR Jakob


Adds a "Select Car" button to the Garage for changing the car model.

Port of Car Switcher 1.0.7 (PolyTrack 0.6.2) to PolyTrack 0.6.3, bundled with the PolyRanked game copy.
Cars that keep the original collision shape count as vanilla physics; a car with a different collision model is reported to PolyModLoader as a physics change.
