PolyRanked

Trackmania-style ranked matchmaking for PolyTrack.

  • Queue for Ranked 1v1, Ranked 3v3 or Ranked FFA from the main menu.
  • Rounds are played live against other players on the same track; first to win enough rounds takes the match.
  • Elo-style rating per queue with divisions from Bronze to Trackmaster.
  • An always-on Quickplay Cup played like PolyCup: open Multiplayer and press Join the Cup – join and leave whenever you like, warmup on every new track (press Ready when done), live rounds with 3-2-1-GO, 10 s for the others once someone finishes, T / Backspace retires, finished racers watch the rest, new track every four rounds, first to 120 points wins and the next cup starts by itself.

Requires a PolyRanked server. Set the server address under Ranked → Settings.
